Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Version 2026-10

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Jordan Migues (Hispana Percusión), based in the Argentine Republic. For any question about your data you can write to hispanapercusion@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

  • Registration data: name or nickname, email, what you play (guitarist, percussionist, etc.) and your level.
  • Your privacy preferences: whether you accepted this policy, whether you want to receive news and whether you allow statistics, with the date.
  • Feedback you send us from the app, if you choose to.
  • Usage statistics (which screens and rhythms are used, type of device), only if you allow them.
  • Data stored on your device so the app works: your profile, your settings, your practice log (streak) and your PRO access. This information does not leave your phone or computer except as described above.

We do not collect your card or payment details: purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the official seller and has its own privacy policy.

3. What we use it for

  • To give you access to the app and, where applicable, to the PRO version.
  • To answer your questions and give you support.
  • To improve the app based on your feedback and, if you allow it, on usage statistics.
  • To send you news, lessons and offers only if you agreed to it. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

4. Legal basis

We process your data with your consent, which you give when you register, and as needed to provide the service, in accordance with Law No. 25,326 on the Protection of Personal Data of the Argentine Republic and, for users in the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

5. Where it is stored and who helps us

  • Google Firebase: stores registrations and feedback (servers in São Paulo, Brazil) and, if you allow it, statistics (Google Analytics).
  • Lemon Squeezy (USA): processes purchases and issues license keys.
  • YouTube: plays the PRO videos.
  • Google Fonts: serves the app's fonts.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app.

Some of these services are outside Argentina, so your data may be transferred to other countries with appropriate safeguards.

6. Cookies and storage on your device

The app stores in your browser the information it needs to work (profile, settings and PRO access). We do not use advertising cookies. Google Analytics only uses cookies if you allow statistics; you can change this at any time from Your profile.

7. How long we keep it

For as long as you use the app or until you ask us to delete it. Statistics are kept in aggregated form for a maximum of 14 months.

8. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can access your data, correct it, update it or ask us to delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time.

  • From the app: Your profile → Privacy and data → Request deletion of my data.
  • Or by writing to hispanapercusion@gmail.com from the email you registered with.

We will answer access requests within a maximum of 10 calendar days, and correction or deletion requests within a maximum of 5 business days.

The data subject has the right to access their personal data free of charge at intervals of no less than six months, unless a legitimate interest is shown, as established in article 14, paragraph 3 of Law No. 25,326. The AGENCIA DE ACCESO A LA INFORMACIÓN PÚBLICA (Argentine Agency for Access to Public Information), as the supervisory authority under Law No. 25,326, is empowered to handle complaints from anyone whose rights are affected by non-compliance with the personal data protection rules.

9. Minors

If you are under 13, you need permission from your parent or guardian to register.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change anything important, we will let you know in the app. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.
