Right of withdrawal · Argentine Law No. 24,240, art. 34 · Resolution 424/2020 of the Secretariat of Domestic Trade

Changed your mind?

You have 10 calendar days from your purchase of HispanApp PRO to cancel it, without giving any reason and at no cost. You do not need to register or log in.

Press the button and send the email from the email address you used to buy:

Cancel my purchase

If the button does not open your email app, write to hispanapercusion@gmail.com with the subject “Purchase withdrawal” and include your purchase email.

What happens next?

  • We will confirm receipt of your request by email, with an identification code, within 24 hours.
  • We will refund the amount through the same payment method.
  • Your PRO license will be deactivated.
